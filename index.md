# Fretflare

**See the fretboard. Play the changes.**

Fretflare is the complete companion for self-taught guitarists: a realistic
fretboard, 31 scales with position boxes, a chord lab, a progression analyzer,
a changes trainer with live microphone feedback that scores your improvisation,
a tuner, training games and structured lessons. English & Spanish. 100% offline.
